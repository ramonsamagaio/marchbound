# Frontier Discovery & Obsession Systems

Marchbound's local 192×192 territories are not intended to be empty traversal space. Frontier Discovery turns exploration into a persistent campaign-writing layer.

## Core rule

A discovery must change at least one of these:

- the current expedition,
- the equipped weapon,
- the Warband,
- the world-map route,
- or the long-term campaign state.

Ordinary chests do not count as discoveries.

## Systems

### Frontier Discoveries
Each expedition procedurally places several anomaly POIs in the local map. Rarity is affected by Wonder and Atlas level. Rumored discoveries are guaranteed to appear in the referenced territory.

Shipped discovery families include Echo Forge, Warden Grave, Greedy Idol, Moon Shrine, Beast Nest, Shattered Caravan, Time Scar, Black Mirror and Sealed Vault.

### Rumors
Dawnkeep maintains one active specific rumor pointing to an accessible frontier coordinate and a named anomaly. Proving it grants Renown and advances a Rumor streak. Certain discovery choices immediately create a fresh rumor, producing chained exploration goals.

### Atlas Insight / Wonder / Codex
Discovering new anomaly types fills a persistent Codex and grants Atlas Insight. Novel discoveries increase Wonder. Higher Atlas/Wonder improve the odds of rare and epic anomalies rather than simply adding passive stat percentages.

### Campaign Marks
Some powerful choices leave persistent tradeoffs. Examples include Blood-Tempered, Moon Mark, Hunger Debt and Time Debt. Time Scars can erase a Mark, making campaign history partially editable rather than a permanent punishment.

### Weapon Echoes
Frontier anomalies can add up to four permanent Echo traits to the currently equipped weapon. Echoes modify future expeditions and live next to Weapon Memory history, giving old weapons reasons to remain emotionally and mechanically valuable.

### Warband Legacies
Units can inherit persistent traditions from discoveries such as Oathkeeper, Beastfriend and Roadborn. Legacies affect expedition behavior through army damage, cadence, mobility and other systemic bonuses.

## Design intent

The loop is designed around self-generated temptation:

rumor → specific territory → local exploration → anomaly choice → permanent consequence → altered build → new rumor / stronger curiosity → next march.

This is intentionally gameplay-driven. It avoids energy timers, login punishment, monetized randomness and fake urgency.
